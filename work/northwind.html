Northwind

A concept marketing site for an analytics SaaS — clear product storytelling, a confident type system and a conversion-first layout that earns the free-trial click.

the brief.

SaaS homepages tend to drown in features. Northwind was a deliberate exercise in the opposite discipline: say what the product does in one line, show it, and make the next step obvious — all above the fold.

Where Éternel is about atmosphere, Northwind is about clarity and trust. Different brief, different world: a cool, precise, product-led system instead of dark-luxury mood.

our approach.

We led with a single value-proposition headline and an accent-blue system that carries the eye from promise → proof → action.

  • One promise, up top. A benefit-led headline and a two-button hero — start free, or book a demo.
  • Proof, fast. A logo strip and product shot within the first screen to earn trust before scrolling.
  • A real UI kit. Buttons, chips, cards and tags defined as a reusable system, not one-off pieces.
  • Conversion-first rhythm. Every section ends on a clear next step — no dead-ends.
“If a visitor can't say what you do and what to click within five seconds, the design has failed — no matter how it looks.”
Credo — design principle behind Northwind

what it shows.

Northwind is our reference for product and startup clients — proof that we can turn a feature list into a clear, credible story that moves a visitor toward signup.
