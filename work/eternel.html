Éternel

A concept identity and single-page site for a modern fashion house — dark-luxury art direction, editorial typography and cinematic smooth-scroll motion.

the brief.

Most fashion sites shout. We wanted the opposite: a homepage that feels like walking into a quiet, expensive room. The goal for Éternel was to prove that a single, well-directed page can carry an entire brand — no mega-menu, no clutter, just atmosphere and one clear invitation.

The challenge was restraint. Dark-luxury goes wrong the moment it feels like a template. Every choice — the exact off-black, the serif's italic, the pace of the scroll — had to earn its place.

our approach.

We built the whole page around a single serif headline and a palette of near-black, bone and a warm metallic. Everything else is negative space and timing.

  • Type as the hero. A large editorial serif set in mixed case, paired with a wide-tracked sans for labels — the only two voices on the page.
  • Motion with intent. Inertia scroll and slow reveals so the page unfolds like a lookbook, never a slideshow.
  • One action. A single "Enter the House" call, repeated only where it earns attention.
  • Detail obsessed. Hairline rules, corner meta, and grain to give the screen a printed, tactile feel.
“Luxury online isn't about adding gold — it's about how much you're confident enough to leave out.”
Credo — design principle behind Éternel

what it shows.

Éternel is the reference we point clients to when they want "premium" without a definition. It demonstrates the level of art direction, typography and front-end craft we bring to identity-led sites.
