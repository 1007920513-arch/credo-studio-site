Questions

good to know.

The things clients ask before we start. Anything else — just ask.

What does a project cost?+
Most landing pages start around $800–$1,500, full multi-page brand sites from $2,500. Every project gets a fixed quote after the discovery call — no hourly surprises. Branding and motion are quoted per scope.
How long does it take?+
A focused landing page is usually 1–2 weeks. A full brand site is typically 3–5 weeks depending on page count, content readiness and rounds of feedback. We agree the timeline up front in the scope.
Do you write the copy and provide images?+
We can. Copy direction and art direction are part of what we do — we'll either shape your existing content or write and source visuals for you as an add-on. Nothing ships with lorem ipsum.
Can I edit the site myself after launch?+
Yes — if you need to update content, we build on a lightweight CMS so you can edit text and images without touching code. If you'd rather we handle changes, we offer a simple monthly care plan.
What do you need from me to start?+
A short brief on your business and goals, any brand assets you already have, and examples of sites you like. We handle the rest and guide you through each stage.
